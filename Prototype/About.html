WHO WE ARE

Meet JCR Drones.

JCR Drones is a locally owned and operated drone spraying business based in Hokitika, West Coast, New Zealand.

Our mission is to help farmers, landowners and businesses manage their land efficiently with modern drone technology.

We aim to provide a professional and reliable service, offering practical spraying solutions for suitable properties and terrain.

We understand that every property is different. That's why we consider the needs of each job and work towards an approach that suits the site and its requirements.
