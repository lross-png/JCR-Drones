About the System

JCR Drones combines the cordination from, flying drones in with being able to lighten a famers load. Here at JCR Drones we aspire to help farmers and lighten their load for them so they may sit back and relax. Locally owned and operated in Hokitika, West Coast Drone Spraying provides professional, efficient and reliable drone spraying services throughout Hokitika and the wider West Coast region of New Zealand. We use modern drone technology to deliver precise and targeted spraying solutions across a range of properties and terrain, helping landowners, farmers and businesses manage their land efficiently. Our services are suited to areas that may be difficult to access using traditional spraying methods, offering a practical alternative that can reduce ground disturbance and improve application efficiency. As a locally owned business, we take pride in supporting our West Coast community with dependable service, quality workmanship and a strong focus on safety, environmental responsibility and customer satisfaction. Fully licensed and authorised for our approved operations, we are committed to meeting all applicable aviation and spraying requirements, ensuring every job is carried out professionally and responsibly. Whether you're a farmer, landowner, contractor or business owner, West Coast Drone Spraying is here to provide a modern, reliable solution tailored to your property and needs. Local knowledge. Professional service. Precision from above. Contact us today to discuss your spraying requirements and find out how drone technology can work for you.
